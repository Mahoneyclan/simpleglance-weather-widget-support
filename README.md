# SimpleGlance Weather Widget support

## About the app

SimpleGlance Weather is a clean, minimal weather widget for Garmin fenix watches.

- Live conditions for two places, where you are and a home location you set, switched with one button.
- Temperature, feels-like, high and low, rain and wind, plus an hourly and a 4-day forecast.
- A glance view showing both places at once, refreshed in the background.
- Weather from Open-Meteo; no account or key needed. Available in the Garmin Connect IQ Store.

## Get help

Questions, problems or ideas? Email **[mahoneyclanapps+simpleglanceweather@gmail.com](mailto:mahoneyclanapps+simpleglanceweather@gmail.com)**.

To help us answer quickly, please include:

- what you were doing and what happened
- your watch model and its software version (on the watch: Settings → System → About)
- the app version (shown in the Connect IQ Store or the Garmin Connect app)
- a screenshot, if it helps

We read every message, and usually reply within a few days.
